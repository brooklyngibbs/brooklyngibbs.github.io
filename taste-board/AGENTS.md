# Taste board curation

Read Brooklyn's exact goal in [PROJECT.md](PROJECT.md). Review the entire
web after each new batch or requested semantic reorganization. Revisit existing
placements as new connections emerge, including when a pin arrives without notes.

The product goal is a growing tool for discovering Brooklyn's taste: eventually
Pinterest references can be added from the site and the whole board can be
reanalyzed there. Until that integration exists, imports and semantic analysis
happen in chat. Never imply the current Reorganize button performs AI analysis.

- Read all active images' exact notes and inspect the new images. Revisit earlier
  images when a connection depends on their visual details.
- Compare recurring subjects, shapes, palettes, lettering, materials, moods,
  remembered media and the specific details Brooklyn points out. Distinguish a
  visible observation or explicit preference from a tentative interpretation.
- Assess recurrence across batches together with the strength of Brooklyn's
  wording and personal memories. Repeated references can justify a larger or
  earlier branch; a singular explicit favorite still matters. Keep overlapping
  evidence counts transparent and do not present a numerical taste score as fact.
- Reconsider the whole hierarchy, image discovery paths, category memberships,
  crossovers, representative pictures, reveal depths and spatial placement.
  Split, merge, rename, promote or move branches when the evidence warrants it.
  Treat every new batch as a whole-board curation pass, including meaning and
  relative emphasis. Retain useful structure when it still fits and record why;
  do not restrict the review to the new images or reshuffle gratuitously.
- There is no fixed set of main categories and no required overarching theme.
  A reference without a convincing route starts its own visible path. Small
  seeds can remain independent until later references suggest a connection.
- Build each discovery route from its category ancestry and its representative
  image. Check that a sibling subject never becomes a required step. Wonderland
  flowers and Bread-and-butterflies each open directly from Alice.
- Choose a primary path for navigation and preserve specific crossovers. Do not
  attach two otherwise unrelated aesthetics merely because both involve music,
  animation, nostalgia or a broad medium. Do not let a color crossover relocate
  an entire subject group.
- Each import must update `organization-review.json` with the complete reviewed
  ID set, evidence for the current groups, decisions, and unresolved hypotheses.
  This is a curation record, not a claim that the local Reorganize button analyzes
  images. That button reloads the curated collection and arranges it.
- Copy user notes verbatim, with `noteAuthor: "user"`. Missing notes display
  `No note`. Never put assistant analysis, attribution corrections or summaries
  in a personal note. Keep Pinterest sources and existing local edits intact.
- Keep the title `taste board`, white dot grid, simple typography, stable smooth
  zoom, compact clusters and modest gaps between distinct paths. Pack small
  groups together and scale their captions with them. Keep detail images
  recognizable when they first appear, with a modest size difference between
  covers and supporting references. Repack when the
  collection changes, with stable positions during zoom. Reveal more
  specific ideas with deeper zoom; do not cap the structure at two levels.
- Use size and reveal depth to express the current evidence for Brooklyn’s
  brand. Give every independent theme a large opening cover, even if it has
  few references. Reveal Alice in Wonderland as a prominent group within
  Make-believe. Keep independent themes separate until there is a meaningful
  parent relationship. Lateral overlaps can connect two independent themes. Zoom progressively
  reveals supporting groups and individual references. Keep
  revealed layers visible until zooming back out. Do not show the whole photo
  collection at once. Let the large opening covers ease toward a size limit
  so more specific pictures can take focus as they grow.
- Revisit each category’s presentation emphasis on every whole-board review.
  Record the evidence for signature and recurring themes in `presentation`.
  Use repeated references, explicit inspiration, and personal memories together.
  A recurring subtheme can appear earlier and larger while keeping its ancestry
  and overlaps. As new evidence arrives, promote or demote themes and revisit
  the layout. Keep early interpretations open as Brooklyn develops her brand.
- Keep pictures of the same subject tightly grouped, including Star Trek and
  clovers. Lines explain different branches and secondary overlaps. A crossover
  may sit at its subject's edge without scattering the rest of that subject.
- Validate all routes and assets, run the existing board checks, and inspect the
  changed paths in the local preview. Preserve a dated snapshot before substantial
  reorganization. Work stays local until Brooklyn requests a push.
